Social Media Safety

Protect your account

  • Turn on two-step verification.
  • Never log in through links sent by others.
  • Log out of shared or public devices.

Protect your privacy

  • Keep your profile private if you can.
  • Don't post your phone number, address or travel plans.
  • Accept friend requests only from people you know.

If something goes wrong

  • Change your password right away.
  • Report the fake or hacked profile on the platform.
  • Tell your friends not to trust messages sent from it.
