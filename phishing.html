Phishing Awareness

Phishing is when someone pretends to be a bank, company or friend to trick you into sharing passwords, OTPs or money.

Warning signs

  • Urgent threats ("your account will be blocked today").
  • Links that don't match the real website name.
  • Requests for OTP, PIN or password.
  • Spelling mistakes and unknown sender numbers.
