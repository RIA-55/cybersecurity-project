Common Online Scams

Fake UPI payment request

You get a "collect request" and are told to enter your PIN to receive money. Entering a PIN only ever sends money out.

Stay safe: never enter your PIN to receive money.

Fake job or lottery offers

You're asked to pay a registration fee for a job or prize that doesn't exist.

Stay safe: genuine employers don't ask candidates for money.

Fake customer care numbers

A number found through a search result pretends to be your bank or delivery company and asks you to install a screen-sharing app.

Stay safe: use the number printed on your card or the official app.
